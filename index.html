I got into journalism the way most people get into things they can't stop doing — by accident, then completely. A campus paper assignment about a canteen price hike turned into a habit of noticing what nobody else was writing about.

Since then I've covered civic negligence, campus culture, and the small, human stories that usually get cut for space. I care less about being first with a story and more about getting it right — the quote in context, the number checked twice, the person who rarely gets asked.

I'm currently looking for reporting opportunities, internships, and stories worth chasing.
